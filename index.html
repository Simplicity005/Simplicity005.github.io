About Me

I am a Software Engineer driven by a curiosity for how complex systems tick—whether that’s a backend architecture or a high-performance Formula 1 engine. From implementing stochastic models for financial markets to optimizing algorithms like Huffman coding, I enjoy bridging the gap between theoretical computer science and practical, hardware-integrated solutions. When I’m not deep in a terminal or tinkering with embedded systems, you’ll likely find me tracking practice sessions on the paddock or organizing local badminton tournaments. I believe in the power of simplicity, both in code and in life.
